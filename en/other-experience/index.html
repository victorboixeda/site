2025 · Solo project · Project lead

Hourglass Outpost

Sea of Thieves had no official competition, and many players were looking for teammates at their level. So in 2025, I launched a competitive project on my own, independent of Sea of Thieves France.

It was the first project I ran by myself. I set the rules and the format, created the visual identity, coordinated ten volunteers and managed the Discord server. I also convinced two streamers, with around 30,000 and 10,000 followers, to partner with the event.

For The Outpost Showmatch, players were selected through a whitelist and the rules were written in French and English. Three hours of preparation led to three hours of play, which I commentated live on Twitch.

It taught me to delegate, to stay firm but fair, and to hold deadlines despite the unexpected. The project has been on hold since I stepped away to prepare for my baccalauréat, and I am considering relaunching it with Sea of Thieves France.

School workshop · Team lead

Brand workshop

In these workshops, a brand gives schools across France a real brief, and each team has one week to answer it. The brand’s issues remain confidential.

In the latest workshop, we had to design the packaging for a new product and its communication strategy. I led the team and learned 3D modelling to present the final render. We finished first regionally, then second nationally.

Nov. 2025 – Feb. 2026 · Internship

Student recruitment sales, ISEG Bordeaux

In the admissions and development department, I guided prospective students from the first contact at education fairs all the way to enrolment: calls, open days and application follow-up in Salesforce.

This first sales experience taught me to adapt my pitch to each profile, handle objections and reassure people.
