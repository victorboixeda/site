On the mic: streams and casting

After hosting the community’s live streams, I now commentate its competitive events on my own, in French and in English.

My goal is to make a competition enjoyable for everyone, including viewers who don’t know the game. During matches, I also remain the players’ point of contact.

I bring the chat into the broadcast and use scarcity mechanics (FOMO) to hold attention. That is harder for an organisation than for a solo streamer, who builds attachment more easily.
