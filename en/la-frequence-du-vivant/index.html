Project 01

La Fréquence du Vivant

A biodiversity project that combines walks in nature with real field data. I covered its go-to-market chain: marketing, sales materials, prospecting and sales.

The project

It rests on three offers. The Marches du Vivant invite the public to collect biodiversity data during guided walks. Businesses get analysis tools and mediation games. Finally, Fréquence Jardin tracks the biodiversity of a garden.

The team

There were four of us during the internship. I reported directly to the president, Laurent Tripied.

Territoire Vivant, a mediation game

B2B clients needed a way to run interactive mediation with their own biodiversity data. The management game designed with the team does exactly that: each action raises four gauges (biodiversity, profitability, resilience, attractiveness), while disruptive events such as hail or heatwaves get in the way.

Before designing it, I analysed an existing game, Parcelle Vivante (by the Communauté de communes de l’Estuaire), to identify its friction points, risks and strengths without copying it.

What I learned

  1. Bouncing back from a no.The first rejections taught me to rewrite my pitch, and that in prospecting, volume matters.

  2. Tailoring the pitch.A large wine estate, a struggling one, a company or a local authority don’t expect the same thing: I learned to build a pitch for each.

  3. Bringing solutions.Whenever I spotted a blocker, I sent the president a short note with solutions so he could decide quickly.

  4. Anticipating and working independently.I got into the habit of assessing risks before acting, and only turning to management when it was really useful.

What I take away

This internship confirmed my taste for creative work and marketing. I discovered a knack for negotiation that I still need to develop, and a wish to explore several areas of marketing before specialising.
